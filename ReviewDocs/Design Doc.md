Money as a progression system: (like tech researh? R&D team cost money to hire and keep on staff but allows for unlocks like higher throughput faster transit etc)
More expensive items require more resources and time to produce, creating a sense of progression and achievement for players.

Why are we producing and selling where are we going? What is the end goal? Factario has a space ship building goal, but honestly that really doesn't matter as the fun lives in the process of building and optimizing the factory. The end goal is more of a narrative device to give players a sense of purpose, but the real enjoyment comes from the journey of managing resources, improving efficiency, and overcoming the biters.

Finite resources – Mines deplete, forests regrow slowly, oil fields dry up.
Changing demand – Markets fluctuate and customers change what they want.
Competition – Other companies buy resources, bid on contracts, and compete for customers.
Operating costs – Fuel, wages, maintenance, taxes, loan payments.
Geography – The nearest resources are exhausted, so transportation becomes increasingly important.
Technology progression – New industries require entirely new supply chains.
